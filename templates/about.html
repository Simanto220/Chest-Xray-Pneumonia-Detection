Dual-Branch Feature Fusion Architecture

Why Combine VGG16 & VGG19?

In chest radiography, pneumonia presents with varying subtleties — from faint ground-glass haziness to dense consolidated opacities. Combining both architectures captures both intermediate spatial details and deeper semantic representations.

Deep Feature Extraction & Fusion Pipeline

The input chest radiograph \((224 \times 224 \times 3)\) passes concurrently through frozen ImageNet pre-trained backbones of VGG16 and VGG19. Each branch extracts rich feature representations which are dimensionally reduced via GlobalAveragePooling2D and concatenated into a unified \(1024\)-dimensional feature vector.

[Input Radiograph: 224x224x3] ├───▶ [VGG16 Backbone (13 Conv Layers)] ───▶ [GlobalAvgPool2D (512-d)] ───┐ │ ▼ └───▶ [VGG19 Backbone (16 Conv Layers)] ───▶ [GlobalAvgPool2D (512-d)] ───▶ [Concatenate (1024-d)] │ ▼ [Dense (128, ReLU)] │ ▼ [Dropout (0.30 Rate)] │ ▼ [Dense (1, Sigmoid Output)] 0: NORMAL | 1: PNEUMONIA

The classification head consists of a Dense (128 units, ReLU) layer with a Dropout (0.3) regularization layer to prevent co-adaptation of features, followed by a single Sigmoid neuron output calibrated against class-weighted binary crossentropy.

Optimization & Data Imbalance Handling

Medical imaging datasets typically suffer from significant class imbalance (unequal distribution between normal and infected cases). The training pipeline utilized:

  • Balanced Class Weighting (Scikit-Learn): Penalizes false negatives on pneumonia cases during backpropagation.
  • Geometric Data Augmentation: Random rotations (\(\pm 15^\circ\)), zooming (\(0.2\)), width/height shifts (\(0.1\)), and horizontal flipping.
  • Adaptive Learning Rate Schedule: ReduceLROnPlateau with factor \(0.2\) and patience \(3\) epochs down to \(1\text{e-}7\).
  • Validation Checkpointing: Preserves only model weights achieving peak validation accuracy.

Explainable AI: Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM)

To provide transparency and clinical trust, the model leverages Grad-CAM. The gradients of the target class score with respect to the final convolutional feature maps are pooled to compute neuron importance weights:

\(L_{\text{Grad-CAM}}^{c} = \text{ReLU}\left(\sum_{k} \alpha_k^c A^k\right) \quad \text{where} \quad \alpha_k^c = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{i} \sum_{j} \frac{\partial Y^c}{\partial A_{i,j}^k}\)

This highlights whether the neural network made its diagnostic assessment based on genuine pathological consolidation rather than irrelevant artifacts (such as medical clips, rib edges, or background noise).
